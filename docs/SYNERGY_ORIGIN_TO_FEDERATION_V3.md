# SYNERGY Origin → Federation V3

**As of:** 2026-09-06  
**Observed federation:** 45 repositories  
**Historical source:** «Новая Россия 2025 / Единая интеллектуальная система», conceptual lineage 2018→2025.

This document translates the historical concept into the current engineering federation. Historical percentages, yields, adoption rates and AI uplift factors are preserved only as research/scenario inputs, never as verified production claims.

## Core translation

Historical six-level system:

1. Personal finance and financial freedom.
2. Participant/business network and regional growth.
3. Development fund, liquidity, investing, market making, arbitrage and trading.
4. Science, patents and commercialization.
5. Industry, jobs, infrastructure, agriculture and regional development.
6. Distributed AI governance and optimization.

Modern federation adds the missing engineering layers: accounting truth, settlement, evidence, authority boundaries, network transport, runtime orchestration, post-quantum trust, compute substrate, reproducible science and production verification.

# 45-repository coverage rule

Every one of the 45 observed repositories must have:
1. a unique role;
2. a federation layer;
3. an authority or `NONE`;
4. one or more origin sections;
5. upstream/downstream contracts;
6. maturity/evidence state;
7. a canonical/legacy/R&D classification.

The machine-readable source is `config/repository_federation_v3.yaml`.

# What is explicitly not carried forward as canonical truth

- guaranteed investment yields;
- fixed AI performance uplifts;
- unconstrained exponential adoption;
- recruitment-funded economics;
- internal token valuation as external wealth;
- unverified scientific claims;
- blockchain as accounting authority;
- AI as unrestricted executive authority.

These remain historical hypotheses or research scenarios and must be re-earned by evidence.
