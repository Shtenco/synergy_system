## 4. Business and statecraft principles
- **Subsidiarity:** decisions at the lowest competent level; escalate only cross-entity issues.
- **Procurement integrity:** publish sources, eligibility, scoring, conflicts, rationale and delivery evidence; protect private data.
- **Competition with interoperability:** open standards, independent providers, transparent quality/price comparisons.
- **Cooperation with accountability:** pooled resources require explicit contributions, rights, obligations, dispute and exit rules.
- **Fiscal discipline:** budgets, reserves, credit, money, capital and grants are distinct; internal token appreciation is not public revenue.
- **Due process:** consequential automated decisions require explanation, appeal and human review.
- **Resilience:** offline operation, backups, independent providers, tested recovery and graceful degradation.
- **Evidence before scale:** no national/industrial rollout based on demos or simulated multipliers alone.
